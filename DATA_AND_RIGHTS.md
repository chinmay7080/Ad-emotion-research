# Public repository scope and research boundaries

This repository is a reviewed presentation of academic work. The underlying TVC35, Pitt, AdCumen, TRIBE, and TSAM materials retain their owners' terms. A public code repository does not grant redistribution or commercial-use rights to their media, annotations, participant data, derived feature arrays, or pretrained checkpoints.

## Included

- Original, reviewed analysis and integrity-checking code.
- Synthetic examples and tests that contain no real advertisement or participant record.
- Aggregate results and figures that do not disclose individual ads, participants, predictions, or media.
- Research plans that clearly mark completed, pending, and conditional work.

## Excluded

- Advertisement videos, frames, soundtracks, transcripts, source IDs, and clip- or ad-level labels or predictions.
- fMRI series, participant identifiers or parcel-by-participant records, raw predicted-brain arrays, and feature matrices.
- Third-party model source bundles and weights, cluster logs, access paths, credentials, and private provenance manifests.

The [Pitt ads dataset](https://people.cs.pitt.edu/~kovashka/ads/) and [released TSAM source](https://github.com/gmontana/DecodingViewerEmotions) should be obtained from their owners where the applicable terms allow. The 1,865-ad Pitt analysis subset is smaller than the complete source collection. Survey *Effectiveness* is not a click-through outcome.

No blanket license for this repository's code is asserted here. Anyone seeking to reuse the code, private inputs, or models should obtain the relevant permissions from their respective owners.
